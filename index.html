Work

Take-IELTS — an IELTS practice platform

Reading, Listening and Writing in the real exam format: the same timing, the same question types and the same band table. Writing essays go through a Supabase Edge Function to an AI model and are marked against the four official IELTS criteria. Every attempt is stored and band score progress is charted. Technologies: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase. take-ielts.vercel.app

Satify — a digital SAT practice platform

Full-length digital SAT practice in an interface modelled on the real exam: Math and Reading and Writing modules with their own timers, a calculator, a highlighter, an answer eliminator and mark for review. The exam runs locked in full screen, answer keys never reach the browser and scoring happens on the server. Results come as scaled 400–1600 scores with a domain breakdown and an explanation for every question. Technologies: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase. satify-uz.vercel.app

Atlas — a desktop tool for reading an unfamiliar codebase

It analyses a project and answers the three questions a newcomer actually has: what is this, how is it wired, and where do I start. A dependency map, a reading route and findings. One Swift engine drives both clients — SwiftUI on macOS and Avalonia on Windows — and both read a codebase identically. Technologies: Swift, SwiftUI, Avalonia. atlas-codemap.vercel.app

PrepPlanner — a macOS app for IELTS and SAT preparation

Plan the day in blocks on a timeline, mark each one Done, Partial or Skipped and record the minutes you really spent. Mock results: raw Listening and Reading scores become bands automatically, and SAT sections add up to a total. An error log, habit streaks and analytics charts. Everything stays on the Mac: no accounts, no backend, no third-party code. Technologies: Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, Swift Charts. prepplanner.vercel.app

XIV — an online storefront for a fashion label

A storefront built from a Figma design system: a filtered catalogue, a product page with a gallery and colour and size pickers, a shopping bag, and search that returns results as you type. Responsive down to 393px. Technologies: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. cloth-store-gules.vercel.app

Orpheus — a pixel-faithful build of a Figma design

A designer portfolio rebuilt one to one from its Figma source. Type sizes, letter-spacing, section rhythm and the ambient glow colours come from the design itself rather than from eye-balling it. Dark and light themes; the built page lands within a couple of pixels of the 1728px artboard. Technologies: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion. orpheus-dusky.vercel.app
